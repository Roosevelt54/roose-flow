# Privacy Policy — Roose Flow

_Last updated: 5 September 2026_

Roose Flow does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

The extension has no backend. It makes no network requests to any server operated
by the developer, and contains no analytics, tracking, advertising, or
third-party SDKs.

## What the extension stores

**Your prompts** are held in Chrome's session storage while the side panel is
open. They are never written to disk and never leave your computer. They are
discarded when you close the side panel, and again when you close Chrome.

**Your settings** — auto-download on/off, download folder, serial numbering,
character consistency, and the delay range between generations — are saved in
Chrome's local storage on your own machine so they persist between sessions.
They are never transmitted anywhere.

**Generated images and videos** are saved to your computer, either through
Chrome's downloads API or, if you pick an output folder with the Browse button,
directly into that folder. Choosing a folder grants the extension access to that
folder alone — nothing outside it is read or written, and the grant can be
revoked at any time from Chrome's site settings. The extension does not upload
your renders, copy them, or retain them anywhere else.

## What the extension accesses

On `flow.google.com`, the extension reads and writes the page in order to type
your prompt into the prompt box, submit it, and detect when a render has
finished. It does not read your account details, cookies, or credentials, and it
does not operate on any other site.

## Permissions and why they are needed

- **storage** — save your settings, and hold the prompt queue in memory.
- **downloads** — save finished renders to the folder you pick.
- **sidePanel** — display the extension's interface in Chrome's side panel.
- **debugger** — used for one thing: sending a real mouse click on Flow's send
  button. Flow ignores clicks generated by a page script, so a genuine click has
  to be delivered through Chrome's debugger protocol. It is attached only to the
  Flow tab, only while a batch is running, and detached when the batch stops;
  Chrome shows a "debugging this browser" banner for as long as it is attached.
  Nothing is read from the page through it.
- **host permission for `https://flow.google.com/*`** — the only site the
  extension operates on; required to enter prompts and detect finished renders.

The extension requests no other permissions.

## Data sharing

None. No data is collected, so none is shared, sold, or transferred to third
parties, and none is used for creditworthiness or lending purposes.
